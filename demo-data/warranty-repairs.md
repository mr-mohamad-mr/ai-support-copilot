# Warranty and Repairs

## What the warranty covers

Every electronic product comes with a two-year manufacturer warranty that covers
faults in materials and workmanship. Accidental damage, such as a cracked screen
or liquid damage, is not covered by the warranty.

## Making a warranty claim

To make a warranty claim, contact support with your order number and a short
description of the fault. If the product is faulty we will repair or replace it
free of charge, and we pay for the courier both ways.

## Batteries

Batteries are covered for one year. A battery that holds less than 80% of its
original capacity within that year counts as faulty and will be replaced.

## Paid repairs

For damage outside the warranty we offer paid repairs. Screen repairs usually
take seven to ten working days and you get a quote before any work starts.
