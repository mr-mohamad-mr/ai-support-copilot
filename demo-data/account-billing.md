# Account and Billing

## Changing your password

To reset a forgotten password, choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page
and we will email you a reset link that is valid for one hour. You can change
your password at any time from Account settings.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal. Your card is charged
when your order is dispatched, not when it is placed.

## Invoices

A VAT invoice is attached to every dispatch confirmation email. You can also
download past invoices as PDF files from the Orders page of your account.

## Updating your billing address

Edit your billing address under Account settings, then Addresses. Changes apply
to new orders only; an order that has already been dispatched keeps the address
it was placed with.
