# Shipping FAQ

## Delivery times

Standard delivery takes 3–5 working days in the UK and 5–10 working days
internationally. Express delivery arrives in 1–2 working days in the UK.

## Tracking

Every order includes tracking. You will receive a tracking link by email as
soon as your order leaves the warehouse, usually within 24 hours.

## Delivery problems

If your parcel shows as delivered but you cannot find it, check with
neighbours and your local collection point first, then contact support with
your order number and we will open an investigation with the courier.
